# Legal Imagen

Use this skill when legal study material should become a clear visual prompt for Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, or a comparable image tool.

## Workflow

1. Identify the visual relationship: legal relationship, timeline, process, comparison, or scenario.
2. Preserve legal actors, conditions, exceptions, chronology, and directional relationships.
3. Select a readable composition and an appropriate image-tool style.
4. Produce a structured prompt with subject, layout, labels, style, color semantics, and negative constraints.
5. Run the output through the formatting and quality rules in [the detailed guide](references/prompt-guide-original.md).

## Accuracy

- Do not simplify away statutory conditions or exceptions.
- Keep Chinese legal labels legible and distinct from decorative text.
- Prefer explicit arrows, numbered steps, and visual grouping for relationships.
- When the source is ambiguous, state the ambiguity instead of inventing a legal conclusion.

The reference guide contains the prompt templates, tool-specific advice, color system, examples, and quality checklist.
